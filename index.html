Dear invited guest,

Some stories
were never
published.

You are among twelve fashion enthusiasts selected for a private preview of the AUREL fiftieth-anniversary exhibition. Our catalogue contains fifty years of silhouettes, faces and celebrated names. Yet the final cover has been removed from every record. At 8:17 this morning, I found a note beneath the empty display: “Follow the credits, not the clothes.” I need someone who knows how to look closer.

— Clara Bennett, Junior Archivist · 17 November 2000
